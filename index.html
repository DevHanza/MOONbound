Meet the Lunar Landing Site Navigator

MOONbound is our Lunar Landing Site Navigator. Pick a site and a date window, and it shows where the Sun and Earth sit above the real lunar horizon, hour by hour. That tells you how much power a lander gets, when it can talk to Earth, and how long it has before sunset or Earthset.

How it works

From exploring the Moon to planning your mission. Follow these simple steps to discover the best lunar landing conditions.

  1. Step 01

    Find your landing location.

    Use 3D Moon and scroll to zoom in. Use the crosshair to identify coordinates, browse landing sites in the Sites panel, or enter coordinates using Jump to.

  2. Step 02

    Choose when your mission takes place.

    Drag the timeline to select any date from 2026 to 2030. Your selected date determines the Sun and Earth's positions for your lunar location.

  3. Step 03

    Discover your landing site.

    Scan Site for the terrain horizon analysis. Explore the Sky and Site views and see how the surrounding landscape affects sunlight and Earth visibility.

  4. Step 04

    Results for mission decisions.

    Explore solar forecast, sunlit hours and Earth communication windows. Test your power budget, network coverage, compare locations.
