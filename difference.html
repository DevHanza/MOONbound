Why us?

NASA’s tools give you the data. MOONbound turns it into a go/no-go answer.

Why we’re different

NASA’s tools give you the data. MOONbound turns it into a go/no-go answer for a landing site.

Other tools

  • Quickmap and Moon Trek map the terrain well, but don’t show when the Sun and Earth clear the horizon.
  • Horizons gives exact Sun and Earth positions as tables. You build the skyline yourself.
  • Power and communication answers mean stitching several tools together.
  • A new site or date means doing the work again.

Our Lunar Navigator

  • NASA SPICE positions and real LRO terrain in one tool, for 139 curated sites.
  • A true 360° skyline, scanned at four mast heights (2–20 m).
  • Sunlight, time to sunset and Earthset, and Earth-link windows in seconds.
  • Compare sites side by side. Change date or battery without a rescan.

The tools we build on

These are excellent tools, and we use the same NASA data behind them. Each answers one part of the question. MOONbound puts the parts together for a landing site and a date.

01

Quickmap

Arizona State University · LRO

An interactive map of high-resolution LRO imagery and topography. Excellent for exploring the terrain around a site, especially the rugged south pole.

MOONbound adds: whether the Sun and Earth clear that terrain, and for how long.

Visit Quickmap
02

Moon Trek

NASA

NASA’s 3D lunar viewer. Fly over the surface, read the topography and get a feel for how terrain shapes the horizon at a site.

MOONbound adds: hour-by-hour sunlight and Earth visibility at that site, for any date window.

Visit Moon Trek
03

Horizons

NASA JPL

JPL’s ephemeris service. It gives precise positions of the Sun and Earth as seen from any point on the Moon.

MOONbound adds: turning those positions into sunlight, power and comms windows against the real skyline.

Visit Horizons
